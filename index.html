How layouts are chosen

Images have equal printed area by default, preserving their clipped proportions. Enlarge an individual image in its preview editor. The search tries row breaks and page splits to make all images as large as possible while keeping these relative sizes. Every image appears once. Keep order adjusts rows without reordering. Fast search samples orders; exhaustive search evaluates every permutation and valid row arrangement for up to 8 images. Larger sets should use fast search.
